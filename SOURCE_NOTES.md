## Source framing

The book explicitly states that it is intended for educational, philosophical, metaphysical, and experimental purposes and disclaims warranties concerning real-world quantum systems, emergent AGI behavior, or dimensional recursion.

It also characterizes itself as both a computational artifact and philosophical manuscript, positioned between explanatory mathematics, ritual geometry, and speculative physics.
