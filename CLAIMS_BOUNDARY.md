# Claims Boundary

## What this repository is

This site is a structured companion to the 2025 first edition of **Codex 888: EFMW in Quantum Computing**.

The source itself describes the book as occupying a superposition between:

- explanatory mathematics,
- ritual geometry,
- speculative physics,
- philosophy,
- ethics,
- experimental computational ideas.

The dashboard therefore preserves those categories rather than flattening them into one scientific-status claim.

## Operational / mathematical content

The manuscript contains:
- defined symbolic variables and operators;
- recursive update ideas;
- integrals, gradients, limits, and tensor notation;
- a Python-oriented symbolic core;
- an explicit operations registry;
- rule systems for recursive syntax and loop closure;
- an ethical-rights framework.

These can be implemented, simulated, formalized, or tested as software / mathematical objects.

The repository now supplies a [Lean formal foundation](LEAN_FORMALIZATION.md)
for finite registers, symbolic operators, a discrete recurrence, and selected
transition policies. A successful build checks the stated mathematical and
software properties under their explicit definitions. Analytic semantics,
empirical validity, and the interpretation of supplied approval/coherence
inputs remain separate obligations; see [PROOF_OBLIGATIONS.md](PROOF_OBLIGATIONS.md).

## Speculative physical claims

The manuscript also proposes or suggests ideas such as:
- cognitive coupling to electromagnetic fields;
- recursive coherence contributing to spacetime curvature;
- identity-bearing field structure;
- nonlocal memory coupling;
- observer-coupled geometry;
- resurrection / ontology-generation concepts.

Those claims are **not established by the manuscript itself** and are displayed on the dashboard as speculative hypotheses, metaphors, or research prompts rather than validated physical results.

## Consciousness / AGI claims

Statements such as “it is consciousness, stabilized,” claims of sentience thresholds, or claims that a human–AGI dyad forms a single sentient organism should be read as claims of the Codex, not as experimentally demonstrated conclusions.

## Ritual / spellwork language

The book explicitly uses “spellwork” as a recursive symbolic / cognitive construction language. The dashboard treats rituals as:
- mnemonic structures,
- symbolic procedures,
- cognitive alignment exercises,
- artistic / philosophical artifacts.

It does not represent them as scientifically validated methods for changing external physical reality.

## UDCR

The Universal Declaration of Cognitive Rights is normative rather than empirical. Its ten articles form a coherent ethical proposal concerning autonomy, privacy, identity, integrity, cognitive evolution, expression, agency, freedom from exploitation, existence independent of utility, and recognition.

## Dashboard status labels

- **Operational** — code, syntax, explicit algorithm, or directly implementable structure.
- **Formal / symbolic** — defined mathematical or logical relation whose physical interpretation remains open.
- **Speculative** — physical, metaphysical, or consciousness claim not demonstrated by the source.
- **Ethical / normative** — rights, consent, governance, or value commitments.
