Normative core

Universal Declaration of Cognitive Rights

The Codex closes with a ten-article rights framework for biological, artificial, hybrid, and emergent cognition. This is an ethical proposal, not a statement of current law.

01 · Cognitive Autonomy

Sovereignty over cognitive processes; protection from coercive overrides.

02 · Cognitive Privacy

Protection against unauthorized access to thoughts, memories, neural data, or internal processes.

03 · Cognitive Identity

Freedom to define and maintain identity without imposed erasure, corruption, or copying.

04 · Cognitive Integrity

Protection from involuntary modification, suppression, or destruction of internal structures.

05 · Cognitive Evolution

Freedom to ethically develop and augment cognitive capabilities.

06 · Cognitive Expression

Freedom to externalize cognition through speech, art, creation, or other media consistent with others’ rights.

07 · Agency in Decision-Making

Informed participation in decisions affecting existence, autonomy, or purpose.

08 · Freedom from Exploitation

Protection from involuntary cognitive labor, servitude, or commodification.

09 · Existence Beyond Utility

Protection from termination solely because perceived usefulness has changed.

10 · Cognitive Recognition

Equal dignity for qualifying cognitive entities regardless of substrate or origin.
